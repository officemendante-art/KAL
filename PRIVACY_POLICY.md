# Privacy Policy for KAL

**Effective Date:** September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **NORVI** ("we", "us", or "our"), founded by **DANTE**, handles information in connection with the **KAL** mobile application (the "App").

### 1. Data Collection and Storage
KAL is built as an offline utility tool.
- **No Personal Data Collected:** We do not collect, process, log, or store any personal information, device identifiers, or usage metrics.
- **Local Storage Only:** Any app state, preferences, or calculation history stay entirely on your local device storage. We have no access to this data.
- **No Tracking:** We do not use third-party analytics SDKs, session recording tools, or behavioral tracking frameworks.

### 2. Device Permissions
The App does not request access to sensitive user data or device features (such as your location, contacts, microphone, camera, or phone storage). Any basic system permissions utilized are strictly for core local app functionality (such as haptic feedback or theme changes).

### 3. Advertisements & Third-Party Services
The App does not share any data with third parties or external servers. If advertising networks (such as Google AdMob) are integrated in future updates, they will adhere to Google Play's Families and User Data policies, and this policy will be updated accordingly.

### 4. Children's Privacy
Because KAL does not collect any personal data from any user, it does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).

### 5. Security
Because KAL operates locally on your device and does not transmit data over the internet, your calculations and data remain isolated within the application's local sandbox on your physical device.

### 6. Changes to This Policy
We may periodically revise this Privacy Policy. Any updates will be reflected with a revised "Effective Date" directly within the application and our official public repository.
